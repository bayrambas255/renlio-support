Frequently Asked

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored primarily on your device. iCloud sync is optional and only operates under your own Apple ID.

How do I delete my account and data?

From inside the app, go to Settings → Delete Account for one-tap removal. Deletion completes within 30 days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions and cancel anytime. You keep premium access until the end of the current period.

My purchased subscription is not restoring

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID, then go to Settings → Restore Purchases inside the app. If the issue persists, contact us.

I found a bug or have a feature request

We'd love to hear from you. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a screenshot if possible.

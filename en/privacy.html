9. Security Measures

We apply administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data:

Technical:

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption end-to-end for all network traffic
  • AES-256 encryption on-device for sensitive data (tokens, credentials)
  • Apple Keychain (Secure Enclave) for credential storage
  • Encryption-at-rest in server-side databases
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Optional Face ID / Touch ID app lock

Administrative:

  • Access on a "need-to-know" basis
  • Annual data protection training for staff
  • DPAs signed with all vendors
  • KVKK VERBİS registration where applicable

Breach notification:

  • KVKK Article 12: Within 72 hours to the Authority; immediately to users if high risk
  • GDPR Articles 33–34: Same 72-hour / high-risk threshold

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction of residence, you have the rights below. Submit any request in writing (email) to omerbas283@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days, free of charge.

10.1 GDPR / UK GDPR Rights (EU and UK residents)

  • Right of access (Art. 15)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16)
  • Right to erasure / right to be forgotten (Art. 17)
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — receive your data in machine-readable form (JSON/CSV)
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Rights regarding automated decision-making (Art. 22) — including profiling
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7) at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu (Türkiye))

10.2 CCPA / CPRA Rights (California residents)

  • Right to Know — categories collected, sources, recipients
  • Right to Delete the personal information we collected
  • Right to Correct inaccurate information
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing (note: we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination for exercising any of these rights

10.3 LGPD Rights (Brazilian residents)

Under LGPD Article 18: confirmation, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion, information about sharing, consent withdrawal, and the right to complain.

10.4 PIPL Rights (Chinese residents)

Under PIPL Articles 44–50: information, copy, portability, correction, deletion, restriction, and consent withdrawal.

10.5 KVKK Article 11 Rights (Turkish residents)

  • Learn whether your data is being processed
  • Request information about the purpose and use
  • Know third parties to whom data has been transferred (domestic or international)
  • Request correction if incomplete or incorrect
  • Request deletion or destruction
  • Request that corrections/deletions be communicated to third parties
  • Object to outcomes of fully automated analysis
  • Claim damages caused by unlawful processing

10.6 Account deletion

You can delete your account in-app via Settings → Account → Delete Account with one tap. This is provided per Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v). After deletion:

  • A 30-day rollback window applies (for non-legally-required data)
  • Legally required records (Apple payment) are anonymized and retained for the statutory period
  • Backup snapshots may retain residual records up to 90 days
